# Courses

Phase 08 provides published course discovery and presentation without LMS delivery. Courses use structured duration, decimal fees with explicit currency, enum-backed mode/status/enrollment configuration, normalized outcomes, curriculum modules, lightweight lessons, ordered contributor faculty, FAQs, media, and polymorphic SEO. Nested curriculum, outcomes, faculty, and FAQs are replaced transactionally during administrative updates. Public list/detail payloads are version-cached for one hour and invalidated by management or SEO changes.

Local/testing seeding creates four clearly developmental courses. Production receives no invented course records.
