# E4ENGINEERS — Homepage Design Concepts

Prepared 24 September 2026 for visual direction approval. These are static image prototypes; no production React or Laravel implementation has been started.

## Brief and source grounding

Reviewed the supplied `agent(1).md`, `skill.md`, and homepage concept brief. The workspace contained only `.git` at inspection; no separate Developer Scope of Work was available there. Reviewed https://e4engineers.in/ in the browser. Its visible content includes engineering fundamentals, diploma/student exam preparation, the electrical/electronics fundamentals course, and articles about power-system economics, generator units and smart grids. The existing UI was not used as a layout template.

All five concepts balance subjects, articles, courses, study resources, physical books and journals/publications. Each includes search, account/cart utilities, primary and secondary discovery actions, updates and a full footer. Book prices, authors, covers and unverified catalogue content are illustrative. Do not publish these as verified commercial offerings. Diagrams are visual concept illustrations, not validated educational diagrams.

## 01 — Modern Engineering Knowledge Portal

File: [E4ENGINEERS_Homepage_Concept_01.png](E4ENGINEERS_Homepage_Concept_01.png)

- **Visual philosophy:** An approachable technical reference portal with clear discovery and a calm hierarchy.
- **Color direction:** White, pale steel, deep navy and engineering blue. Intended palette: `#16324F`, `#2463B5`, white.
- **Typography:** Strong modern sans-serif headings, highly legible sans-serif body copy.
- **Hero:** “Engineering knowledge, connected.” Universal search, paired discovery actions and a technical power-network illustration.
- **Section order:** Header → hero/search → eight disciplines → featured knowledge → course → study resources → books → publications → value/updates → footer.
- **Why it suits E4ENGINEERS:** Makes the breadth of the platform understandable early, with subjects as the main organizing principle.
- **Distinctive features:** Search-led opening, prominent subject matrix, one lead article with supporting stories, restrained product presentation.

## 02 — Premium Engineering Education Platform

File: [E4ENGINEERS_Homepage_Concept_02.png](E4ENGINEERS_Homepage_Concept_02.png)

- **Visual philosophy:** An academic catalogue that emphasizes deliberate learning and technical depth.
- **Color direction:** Ivory, navy and muted gold: `#FAF8F3`, `#172C42`, `#B39155`.
- **Typography:** The generated concept uses an academic serif/sans pairing; serif display and content headings add a premium tone.
- **Hero:** “Build deeper engineering understanding.” An exploded gearbox composition gives the page a strong mechanical-engineering identity.
- **Section order:** Header → hero → learning paths → featured courses → discipline index → articles → resources → books → journals → purpose/updates → footer.
- **Why it suits E4ENGINEERS:** Helps learners enter by intent and makes courses visible while preserving the wider publishing and commerce ecosystem.
- **Distinctive features:** Learning-path hierarchy, illustrated mechanical hero, syllabus-like courses, gold resource band and a restrained book shelf.

## 03 — Engineering Digital Library

File: [E4ENGINEERS_Homepage_Concept_03.png](E4ENGINEERS_Homepage_Concept_03.png)

- **Visual philosophy:** A browsable reference collection organized by discipline and content format.
- **Color direction:** White, pale sage, dark green ink and restrained teal: `#F0F5F2`, `#213B36`, `#34796B`.
- **Typography:** Compact modern sans-serif with clear resource labels and metadata.
- **Hero:** “Find your next engineering insight.” Prominent search with example topics and library/subject actions.
- **Section order:** Header → search hero → disciplines → content-format tabs → subject sidebar/articles → study materials → course resources → journals → books → value → recent additions → footer.
- **Why it suits E4ENGINEERS:** Supports visitors who arrive with a specific topic or resource type in mind.
- **Distinctive features:** Visible catalogue taxonomy, content-type tabs, persistent-looking subject index within the discovery section, and publication records.

## 04 — Engineering Marketplace + Knowledge

File: [E4ENGINEERS_Homepage_Concept_04.png](E4ENGINEERS_Homepage_Concept_04.png)

- **Visual philosophy:** Connect learning discovery with practical purchase decisions.
- **Color direction:** White, midnight blue and orange with pale blue surfaces: `#16304B`, `#CA6437`.
- **Typography:** Bold practical sans-serif with strong price and action hierarchy.
- **Hero:** “Knowledge to learn. Resources to build.” A knowledge feature sits beside a dedicated technical-books promotion.
- **Section order:** Search header/navigation → split hero → discovery lanes → disciplines → articles → course/resources → four-book catalogue → academic publications → value → updates → footer.
- **Why it suits E4ENGINEERS:** Introduces commerce immediately while retaining substantial educational content before the product catalogue.
- **Distinctive features:** Two-tier utility header, dual hero destinations, four-column product merchandising, prominent cart actions and paired course/resource content.

## 05 — Editorial Engineering Authority

File: [E4ENGINEERS_Homepage_Concept_05.png](E4ENGINEERS_Homepage_Concept_05.png)

- **Visual philosophy:** A technical publishing identity built around curation and subject expertise.
- **Color direction:** Warm paper, charcoal navy and rust: `#F7F5EF`, `#202C36`, `#A65436`.
- **Typography:** Editorial serif headlines with restrained sans-serif utilities; fine rules and varied column proportions establish hierarchy.
- **Hero:** “Ideas that power engineering.” A detailed turbine drawing anchors the opening spread.
- **Section order:** Masthead → editorial hero → featured articles → numbered discipline index → publication feature → courses → study resources → selected books → purpose/updates → publisher-style footer.
- **Why it suits E4ENGINEERS:** Establishes credibility for technical writing and publications while keeping courses and book sales accessible.
- **Distinctive features:** Oversized editorial headline, numbered subject directory, large journal feature and a light publisher-style footer.

## Comparison and selection

| Concept | First visitor task | Main organizing device | Commerce emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 Portal | Find a subject or topic | Search and subject grid | Integrated supporting section |
| 02 Education | Choose a learning direction | Learning paths and courses | Curated academic shelf |
| 03 Library | Find a particular resource | Format tabs and subject index | Reference catalogue |
| 04 Marketplace | Discover knowledge or shop | Split hero and discovery lanes | Most prominent |
| 05 Editorial | Read curated technical content | Editorial hierarchy and publications | Selected titles |

Choose a primary direction based on how visitors should enter the platform. Subsequent refinement should resolve final typography, real catalogue assets, verified copy and responsive behavior before production development.

## Review notes

All five contain the required content families and complete header-to-footer compositions. There are no customer ratings, invented numerical success claims or generated people. Generated small print and technical illustrations require editorial review before implementation.
